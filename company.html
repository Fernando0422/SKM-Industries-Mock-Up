Company

An engineer who built a shop, then kept improving it

Founder Sanjeev Kapoor started SKM in 1992. A mechanical engineer by training and a programmer by habit, he has treated the shop as a system to be improved ever since.

Valencia, CA
The story

Since 1992, a reputation built part by part

SKM Industries has built a reputation for highly accurate, quality parts, delivered with a high level of customer service and attention.

Today the shop runs 16 CNC centers with collaborative robots on the turning and milling lines, mechanical engineers on staff, and a custom information system that runs the floor without paper.

  • Over 30 years of industry experience
  • AS9100D & ISO 9001 certified
  • A four-day, 36-hour work week, paid for 40
Timeline

Three decades of deliberate change

  1. 1992

    SKM Industries is founded

    Sanjeev Kapoor starts the shop in Valencia, CA, machining accurate parts for local customers.

  2. 2015

    Caltech selects SKM for LIGO

    SKM is chosen to manufacture parts for LIGO and Advanced LIGO, the observatories that later detect gravitational waves for the first time.

  3. 2019

    The automation search begins

    Kapoor starts evaluating automation, looking for robots that could work beside people, without cages, in a shop that changes setups every few days.

  4. Oct 2019

    First collaborative robot

    SKM orders its first Universal Robot through Numatic Engineering, programmed to load machines, run cycles and signal for inspection like an operator.

  5. 2020

    Essential business through the pandemic

    SKM keeps running as orders slow. The automation groundwork laid in 2019 becomes central as the shop reorganizes around a smaller core team.

  6. 2023

    AS9100 and the CNC West profile

    SKM earns AS9100 registration and is profiled in CNC West Magazine as a model of shop-floor automation, reporting better output with six people and five robots.

Why cobots

Robots that stop on contact, priced for a job shop

Traditional robotics needed safety cages and large budgets. Collaborative robots stop on contact, so they work beside people. For a shop that changes setups every few days, that changed the math.

  • Robots run through breaks, lunches and off hours
  • One person monitors three to four machines and acts as QC
  • SKM helped beta-test Universal Robots' remote control software

SKM has described the first cobot's payback at around seven months, at two extra hours of production per day.
