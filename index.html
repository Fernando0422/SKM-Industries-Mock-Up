01 / Capabilities

Turning, milling and inspection under one roof

Sixteen CNC centers and an automated CMM, run through one paperless production system. From first article to final inspection, the evidence follows the part.

Turning

Mazak turning centers

Seven Mazak turning centers with 6 inch chucks, bar feeders, tool touch probes and automatic tool life management.

  • Dimensions within 0.0001 inch in production
  • Collaborative robots for machine tending
Milling

Haas milling centers

Nine Haas milling centers with 4th and 5th axis capability, from DT-1 to VF3SS with travels up to 40 by 20 by 20 inches.

  • 100% part probing for accuracy
  • Tool touch and spindle probes
Inspection

Automated CMM

A fully automated Micro-Vue CMM runs optical and probe inspection for first article, in-process and final checks.

  • Production tolerances at 60-70% of print
  • First article results before production

02 / Proof of work

Parts for the discovery of gravitational waves

In 2015 Caltech selected SKM Industries to manufacture parts for LIGO and Advanced LIGO. Those observatories made the first direct detection of gravitational waves, ripples in spacetime caused by colliding black holes.

LIGO demanded components machined to tolerances commercial work rarely approaches. Caltech selected SKM for LIGO and Advanced LIGO.

LIGO / Caltech
A shop trusted with physics-grade hardware.

When a project needs parts for detecting gravitational waves, the machining standard is set by the physics, not by the print.

Collaborative robots
Robots that keep machines running off-hours.

SKM was an early adopter of collaborative robots, which run through breaks, lunches and extra hours. The shop reported six people and five robots producing more than it did with 17 people.

Paperless shop floor

Every job managed by a custom information system

SKM built its own production information system. It manages and integrates the data behind every customer work order and runs the shop entirely without paper.

  • Quality specs, materials and processes stored per order
  • iPads at every workstation, tied to a central server
  • Complete traceability and document control
  • Repeat orders recall their full history instantly
