Supplier terms & conditions

Clear expectations for the companies that supply SKM Industries, keeping material, certification and delivery quality consistent with the AS9100D system.

01 / The terms

What every SKM supplier agrees to

These are the published supplier requirements (SKM form F 8.4-3). Download the full PDF for the complete revision.

  • Suppliers provide a certificate of conformance and, as applicable, material and processing certifications with every shipment.
  • When the purchase order specifies it, an inspection report must accompany the shipment.
  • When the PO requires it, a documented first article inspection report is submitted to quality before production begins.
  • Suppliers assume financial responsibility for replacement, rework and material if production starts without first article approval. If an AS9102 form is required, it must be stipulated on the PO.
  • Any shipment delay must be communicated to SKM quality as soon as it is known.
  • Changes to contract requirements, product, process, suppliers or facility location require written SKM approval.
