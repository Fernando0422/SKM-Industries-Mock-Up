02 / Quality system

How quality is controlled during the run

Document control

Paperless shop floor

iPad portals at production and quality stations provide instant access to inspection travelers, prints and critical data, live from the ERP server.

Dimension control

60-70% of print tolerance

Production tolerances are set at 60 to 70% of print tolerances so parts stay comfortably inside spec across the run.

Inspection

100% part inspection

Operators inspect every part under 4-10X magnification, checking dimensions, cosmetics, finishes and packaging.

Metrology

Automated CMM

The fully automated Micro-Vue CMM performs rapid first article, in-process and final inspections with optical and probe measurement.

First article

Documented before production

First article inspection reports are documented before production begins, per purchase order requirements.

Traceability

Complete records

Each order carries quality specs, material certifications and process history, stored in the production system and retrievable on demand.

03 / Caltech LIGO

The project that proves the system

Caltech LIGO supplier

In 2015, Caltech selected SKM Industries to manufacture parts for LIGO and Advanced LIGO, the projects behind the first detection of gravitational waves.

Science of that scale does not tolerate loose quality systems. The same inspection discipline and documentation that supported LIGO applies to every order that comes through the door.
