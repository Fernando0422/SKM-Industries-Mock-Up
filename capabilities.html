Capabilities

Sixteen CNC centers. One standard of evidence.

Turning, milling, automated inspection and assembly under one AS9100D certified roof, coordinated by a custom paperless production system.

01 / Machine shop

Equipment that holds a 0.0001 inch standard

SKM keeps current-generation machine tools and upgrades software continuously. Every program is verified in 3-D before material is cut.

TurningMazak turning centers
  • Seven Mazak turning centers with 6 inch chucks and collaborative robots for machine tending
  • Dimensions maintained within 0.0001 inch throughout production runs
  • Bar feeders, tool touch probes and automatic tool life management
  • Capability to eliminate grinding in most applications
MillingHaas milling centers
  • Nine Haas milling centers with 4th and 5th axis capability and collaborative robots
  • Models from DT-1 to VF3SS, with travels up to 40 by 20 by 20 inches
  • Tool touch and spindle probes for accuracy and speed
  • 100% part probing for machining accuracy and inspection
ProgrammingCAD / CAM systems
  • Centralized Virtual Gibbs CAD/CAM system for efficient programming
  • Wireless-enabled machines for on-the-fly program uploads
  • 3-D rendering verifies every program before machining
  • Continuous software upgrades with manufacturer support
InspectionAutomated CMM
  • Fully automated Micro-Vue CMM for optical and probe inspection
  • Rapid first article, in-process and final inspections
  • Programs written for fast, repeatable inspection results
AssemblyHigh-precision assemblies
  • Machined metal and plastic components with assembly under one roof
  • Full traceability from raw material to finished assembly
  • Repeat orders recall stored quality specs, materials and processes

03 / Production system

A paperless shop floor with complete traceability

Document control

iPad portals

iPad portals at production and quality stations give instant access to inspection travelers, prints and critical information.

Live documents

ERP server access

Live document access from the ERP server removes the revision-control problems of paper.

Information management

Every order, organized

Quality specs, materials and processes are stored per order and recalled instantly for repeat work.

04 / Production-run quality

Quality control during the run, not only after it

Dimension control

60-70% of print tolerance

Production tolerances are set at 60 to 70% of print tolerances so parts stay inside spec through the entire run.

Operator inspection

100% part inspection

Every part is inspected by operators under 4-10X magnification, covering dimensions, cosmetics, finishes and packaging.

First article

CMM first articles

First articles move through the automated CMM quickly, so production approval never waits on inspection.
